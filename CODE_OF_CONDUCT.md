# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project respectful,
professional, and welcoming.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus technical feedback on the work, not the person.
- Prefer clear evidence, reproducible examples, and practical tradeoffs.
- Respect privacy and do not share confidential information.
- Handle security concerns responsibly and privately.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Sharing secrets, production data, or exploit details in public issues.
- Repeated disruption of technical discussion.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments, issues, pull requests, or other
contributions that violate this code of conduct. Serious or repeated violations
may result in temporary or permanent restriction from project participation.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns to the project maintainers. Security issues should be
reported according to [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
